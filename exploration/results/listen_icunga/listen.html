What changed between the rows. On an isolated word the duration predictor crushes a medial phoneme to 32 ms — a hard 3-frame floor — while stretching the final vowel to about 2.3× the median. lengthScale cannot fix that: it scales every phoneme uniformly, so the imbalance survives. Cutting the word out of the carrier “Iri jambo ni <word>.” using the model’s own alignment gives an even profile instead.

even below is final-phoneme duration ÷ median phoneme duration. 1.00 is even; 2.86 means the last sound takes nearly three times the typical one.
